# Challenge #11: Input - Bio

## Problem

In challenge11.py, write the following program:
Let's get to know our user. Ask them the following questions and in the end show them their answers:

• Where were you born?

• How old are you?

• What is your favourite ice cream flavour?

• What is your favourite movie?

• What is your favourite colour?

In the end, output the following:

Use appropriate variables and substitute the information you get through input in the <<___>> spaces.

## Solutions

```python
name = input("What is your name? ")
city = input("Where were you born? ")
food = input("What is your favourite food? ")
hobby = input("What is your favourite hobby? ")

print("Your name is " + name + ".")
print("You were born in " + city + ".")
print("Your favourite food is " + food + ".")
print("You like to " + hobby + ".")
```
